ZHUBUNTU Guide

Welcome to use ZHUBUNTU!

Zhubuntu isn't the distribution of Ubuntu. It is just an open-source Android desktop environment, like GNOME's style. It is easy-to-use ,always for free, and safe. If you have any problems ,please send me an email 📧 to 972059313@qq.com.

Plenty of Internal Applications for You

There are plenty internal apps developed by us, such as calculator, browser, and novel reader. They're free , stylish , open-source and safe, no advertisement, no virus. You can also add your apps that have installed on your phone to ZHUBUNTU desktop.

Now ,Start to Install It

1. First, click here to download and install ZHUBUNTU app and Any Launcher (by Tumuyan)
2. Second, open Any Launcher, click 选择应用=>优先从后台切换到前台=>Zhubuntu.
3. Third ,click 设置桌面 (if it quit by flashing, please go to System settings=>Application settings=Default Desktop app =>AnyLauncher)
4. Lastly, return to desktop, success!
